DAM-BREAK HYDRODYNAMIC FLOOD SIMULATION

Predict • Simulate • Visualize • Prepare

OBJECTIVE

To simulate dam-break scenarios using hydrodynamic modeling to predict flood propagation, depth, arrival time and impact on downstream areas for better disaster preparedness and decision making.
