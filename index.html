Methodology

Adaptive Multi-Fidelity Flood Engine

How HydroGuard balances computational speed with hydrodynamic accuracy.

01

Fast solver across the full domain

A computationally light solver covers the entire modelled area quickly, giving an initial picture of flood extent, depth and timing.

02

Full 2D shallow water equations where it matters

The breach zone, main channel, steep terrain and populated areas are automatically escalated to the full 2D SWE solver for higher-fidelity results.

03

Adaptive grid refinement

Grid resolution tightens around high-gradient or high-consequence regions instead of using one fixed resolution across the whole domain.

04

Accuracy / physics checks

Intermediate checkpoints compare fast-solver behaviour against physical plausibility thresholds to decide where escalation is needed.

05

Software-only, demo-data prototype

This SIH26161 build demonstrates the workflow and interface using illustrative terrain, hydrology and performance data. It is not connected to a live DEM or hydrological data pipeline, and reports no hardware dependency.

Why adaptive multi-fidelity?

Full physics everywhere: accurate, but computationally heavy across low-consequence areas too.
Fast solver everywhere: quick, but less reliable in critical, rapidly-changing zones.
Adaptive: targets full accuracy where it changes the outcome, speed everywhere else.
